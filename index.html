The Novel

Some summers never quite end.

That summer

Kari and her twin brother Matt are fresh out of high school when their family rents a cottage in the lake town of Culver Point. It should be one last idyllic season before college, until their father's temper turns the summer into something else, and a local boy named Michael changes what Kari thinks she knows about love.

Eight years later

Kari is driving back to Culver Point for a family wedding, her devoted fiancé beside her, knowing nothing of what happened there. The lake looks the same. Nothing else is.

A debut in two timelines about first love, a family's fracture, and the long ripple of one summer, set against the Ontario lakeland.
