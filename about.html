About

Sabrina Fielding is a Toronto-based writer originally from Alliston, Ontario. The Way We Left Things, her debut novel, will be published by Ballantine Books under Jenna Bush Hager's Thousand Voices imprint in June 2027.

Her short story “Knick Knack” won the inaugural Montreal Fiction Prize and was nominated for a 2025 National Magazine Award for Fiction. Her work has also appeared in Yolk Literary, Montréal Writes, The Queen's Journal, and MUSE.

She holds degrees in French and Education from Queen's University and has worked in education and the non-profit sector. Her love of the great Canadian outdoors runs through everything she writes.
